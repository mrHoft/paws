## Nimble Paws
Nimble Paws is a charming arcade game, made for GameJam2025. Help a clever cat overcome obstacles and catch prey in a world where everything comes down to one perfectly timed jump!

The game offers a unique experience for both solo players and fun company: multiplayer is available on desktop and TV, letting you compete with friends on a single screen.

### Gameplay
The controls are maximally simple and intuitive, so anyone can jump right in:
- One Button: Press and hold SPACEBAR, LEFT MOUSE BUTTON, a gamepad button, or just TAP the screen. The longer you hold, the higher and further your cat will jump.
- Catching Prey: Land directly on a target to catch it and earn points. But hurry—if you take too long to aim, your prey will escape.
- Obstacles: Cleverly jump over obstacles. A collision will send your cat back to the start and reduce your score, so plan your trajectory carefully!
- Combo System: Catch critters one after another without stopping to build a combo and multiply your points.

### Key Features
- Multiplayer: Compete with friends on a single PC or TV. The game fully supports multiple gamepads, as well as keyboards and mice for a party-ready experience.
- Deep Upgrade System: Enhance your cat's skills to tackle levels more effectively. Boost running speed, and jump power and distance to catch rarer prey and achieve new record scores.
- Global Leaderboards: Prove your skill against players worldwide. Strategic use of upgrades is the key to climbing the ranks and becoming the most nimble cat of all.
- Hidden Achievements & Easter Eggs: Explore every level to uncover all the secrets. The developer has hidden numerous playful references, and the achievement system will motivate you to become a true feline explorer.
- Play Your Way: Enjoy the game however you like—whether on a keyboard, mouse, gamepad, or touch screen.
